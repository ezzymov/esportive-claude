# Game Pitch

**Working title:** eSports Tycoon
**Platform:** Roblox (mobile-first, also PC/console)
**Genre:** Tycoon / management / light-gacha / minigames

## One-liner
Start with a cheap PC and one rookie in a garage. Hire talent, open cases, upgrade rigs, win tournaments, go viral with clips, and build a world-champion franchise.

## Pillars
1. **Always something to unlock** – short-term (a case) and long-term (a Worlds trophy) goals overlap.
2. **You are the org, and also a player** – manage *and* play minigames that directly feed the economy.
3. **Spectacle** – wins, clips and case reveals must *look and sound* exciting.
4. **Show it off** – your HQ, roster, jerseys and clip views are visible to friends and visitors.
5. **Fair & readable** – odds shown, no pay-to-win walls, clear numbers.

## Core loop
```
Earn $ (passive income + minigames + match wins + sponsors)
   → Spend: open cases / hire players / upgrade PCs / expand HQ
   → Stronger roster plays scrims & tournaments
   → Wins + highlight clips → Fame & Followers
   → Fame unlocks sponsors, leagues, bigger payouts
   → repeat, then Franchise Rebirth for permanent bonuses
```
See [[Progression Guide]] for the stage-by-stage version.

## Target audience
> **Update 2026-10-20:** the shooters now use realistic firearms (no blood, ragdoll deaths). Complete Roblox's content maturity questionnaire; the label it gives decides the real minimum age. Expect a teen-and-up audience (roughly 13+) rather than 9+.

Teens and up (13+, final age set by the Roblox maturity label) who like tycoons (e.g. Retail/Gym/Restaurant Tycoon style), case-opening games, and watching/playing competitive games.

## Monetization (keep ethical)
- Gamepasses: 2× Cash, Auto-Collect, VIP Lounge, Extra Roster Slot
- Robux-optional premium cases (odds shown, free-currency path always available)
- Cosmetics: jerseys, logos, desk skins, victory emotes
- ⚠️ Check Roblox's current policy on paid random items (odds disclosure, regional/age limits) **before** shipping paid cases.

## IP safety
Use **fictional game titles and brands** (no real esports orgs, game logos, or hardware brand names). Example fictional hardware brands: *NovaCore, Vektor, Pulse Audio*.
