# Other Features (short specs)

## Plot & HQ
Your plot grows: Garage → Café Corner → Gaming House → Training Center → Studio Campus → Arena. Buttons-to-buy tycoon style (Roblox tycoon staple). Rooms: Practice Floor, Tech Shop, Case Vault, Streaming Booth, Trophy Hall, Bootcamp Dorms, Merch Store, Arena.

## Sponsors
Offers appear at Fame thresholds. Contract = objectives (win 3 matches, reach 50k followers) → payout per week + jersey patch. Cancel penalties. Sponsor tiers: Local shop → Energy drink → Hardware brand → Global tech. (Fictional brands.) Merch store sells jerseys for passive income.

## Staff & Training
Coach (+team power), Analyst (reveals opponent weakness), Manager (auto-collect, offline income), Physio (reduces fatigue), Scout (better free agents), Social Media Manager (clip views). Training Center queues stat training with timers (skippable with Gems).

## Social & Multiplayer
Visit friends' HQs, like their trophy shelf, friend scrims, global/friends leaderboards (Fame, Followers, Trophies), trade offers with tax, shared server events.

## Franchise Rebirth
After winning Worlds: reset cash/roster/plot, keep trophies, cosmetics, followers (partial). Gain **Legacy Points** → permanent multipliers, extra roster slot, new starting perks, new "Region" to start in.

## Daily Loops & Quests
Daily login streak crate, 3 daily quests, weekly challenge, free Rookie Crate every 30 min (timer), offline earnings (capped, boosted by Manager).

## Live Ops & Events
Seasonal cases (Summer Cup, Winter Major), limited jersey, weekly server-wide "Worlds Live" tournament, codes for updates.
