# Progression Guide

The player journey in 10 stages, plus the rebirth loop. Times are **target active play time** for a new player; tune in playtests.

| # | Stage | Fame gate | Unlocks | Main goal | Target time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Garage Squad** | 0 | Intro cutscene, plot, your own station, Founder card, Pickup Games, Aim Trainer, Tech Fix jobs, team creator | Earn your first money solo, hire your first teammate, reach 3 players | 0–15 min |
| 2 | **Café Rookies** | 100 | Rookie Crate, Reaction Test, Tech Shop, 5-slot roster | Open first crates, full roster | 15–45 min |
| 3 | **Local Circuit** | 500 | Scrims, Local Cup, Training Center, leaderboards | Win first trophy | 45 min–2 h |
| 4 | **Rising Org** | 2k | Clip Editor/feed, first sponsor, Pro Crate, friend scrims | First 1k followers | 2–4 h |
| 5 | **Semi-Pro Division** | 8k | Staff hiring, Gaming House, 2nd game title | Promotion to Pro division | 4–8 h |
| 6 | **Pro League** | 30k | Streaming Booth, Merch store, Elite Crate, regional tournaments | Top 4 in a season | 8–15 h |
| 7 | **National Champions** | 100k | Trophy Hall, 3rd title, Academy, transfer market | Win national title | 15–25 h |
| 8 | **International Stage** | 400k | Arena (spectators), Legend Crate, global sponsors | Qualify for Worlds | 25–40 h |
| 9 | **World Contenders** | 1.5M | Mythic parts, Worlds Live event, stadium upgrades | Reach Worlds final | 40–60 h |
| 10 | **World Champions** | 5M | Franchise Rebirth | Win Worlds → Rebirth | 60+ h |

## Rebirth loop (Stage 10+)
Rebirth 1 → new Region, Legacy Points, +25% income, 6th roster slot, new cosmetic tier. Each rebirth needs more Fame for the final gate but moves ~2× faster thanks to perks.

## Each stage should deliver
- [ ] A **new toy** (feature/room) within the first 5 minutes of the stage
- [ ] A **mid-stage goal** (achievement, quest) and **stage boss** (tournament/sponsor deal)
- [ ] A visible HQ change (new room/lighting/banner)
- [ ] A reward cinematic (promotion banner, trophy)

## Pacing rules of thumb
1. First **hire by ~8 minutes**, first **unbox by ~11 minutes**. You start solo as the [[Founder (You)]].
2. Something purchasable every **30–60 seconds** early, every **2–5 minutes** mid-game.
3. Price curve: each tier ≈ 2.2–3× previous; income ≈ 2×. So purchases stretch gradually.
4. Never block progress behind Robux; Robux shortens time (2× cash, skip timers).
5. Offline income capped at 4 h (8 h with gamepass).
6. Every major unlock needs a **tutorial popup** + arrow pointing to it.

## Currencies by stage
- **Cash $** – all stages (income, wages)
- **Fame ★** – gating (never spent; earned by wins/clips)
- **Followers 👥** – gating for sponsors (from clips, stream)
- **Gems 💎** – premium-ish; earned slowly (quests, milestones) + Robux
- **Shards** – duplicates → crafting
- **Legacy Points** – rebirth only
