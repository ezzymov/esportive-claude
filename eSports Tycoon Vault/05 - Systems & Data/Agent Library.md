# Agent Library (Aim Arena)

Agents are the realistic tactical characters your roster players control in [[Aim Arena]], like the agent models in CS2. They are **cosmetic only**: no Agent is stronger than another. Each roster player picks one Agent per side in their Loadout ([[Skin Pattern System]]). Nameplates show on top in matches and clips.

The stylized sci-fi characters that used to be here (Volt, Glitchling, Starling…) are now heroes in [[Spectra]] → [[Hero Library]].

## Design rules
- **Realistic tactical look:** plate carriers, helmets, headsets, gloves, knee pads, camo. Grounded colors.
- **Fictional factions only.** No real military, police or special-forces unit names, patches or insignia (no SEAL, SAS, GIGN, FBI, SWAT lettering). No real-world extremist or terrorist group references or look-alikes, and no ethnic or cultural stereotypes.
- **Side readability:** Raiders always carry an orange/tan accent (armband, patch, helmet band); Wardens a blue accent. Visible from far away.
- **No blood or gore** on any model or skin. Battle-Worn condition = dirt, scuffs and worn fabric only.
- Every Agent: buy-phase idle, side-specific run cycle, celebrate emote, death ragdoll.

## Agent variants (skins)
Agents get camo and patch variants, not costume changes:
- **Camo sets:** Urban, Woodland, Desert, Arctic, Night, Digital, Tiger Stripe, Team Colors.
- **Patch sets:** each faction has 3 patch variants; Championship patches from tournaments.
- **Gloves** are their own skin slot (see [[Aim Arena]] loadout): Sport, Driver, Moto, Hand Wraps, Specialist, Tactical. Rare drops.
- **Voice packs** (callouts like "Rotate B", "Plant down"): per faction, with a Pro Editor-style premium pack ([[Microtransaction Ideas]]).

## Unlocks
- Mako and Vega free from the start.
- Uncommon/Rare Agents from Style Crates and Arena Crates ([[Case Library]]).
- Epic/Legendary from Elite/Legend Crates or Collection rewards.
- Event Agents only during their event.
