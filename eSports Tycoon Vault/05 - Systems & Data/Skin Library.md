## Naming & art rules
- Fictional names only. No real brands, logos, games or CS2 skin names.
- Every skin must read clearly at phone size: bold shapes, high contrast, no tiny text.
- Each pattern needs a seamless tile texture (512×512 or 1024×1024) so seed offsets look good.
- Make 1–2 "hero" skins per collection with animation; the rest static to save performance.
- Before adding a Signature Pattern, render all 1,000 seeds in Studio and pick the best-looking ones.

## Starter target for launch
~60 skins: 8 collections × 5–7 skins + title skins. Add one new collection per big update and 3–6 event skins per event.
