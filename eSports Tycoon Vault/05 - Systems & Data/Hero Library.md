# Hero Library (Spectra)

Heroes for [[Spectra]], the hero shooter. Each hero has a **laser blaster**, 2 abilities and an Ultimate. Heroes matter in the sim through class and team comp. These heroes started as the stylized Aim Arena Operatives; Aim Arena now uses realistic Agents ([[Agent Library]]).

## Design rules
- One big silhouette idea per hero (ears, wings, tank, lantern) so they read at phone size.
- Stylized sci-fi, bright colors. Laser blasters and energy effects. **No blood or gore**; eliminated heroes ragdoll with a short energy flicker.
- Names checked against Overwatch, Valorant, Apex, Paladins, Marvel Rivals and other hero shooters before shipping.
- Every hero: hero-select pose, idle, run variant, ultimate voice line, victory pose.
