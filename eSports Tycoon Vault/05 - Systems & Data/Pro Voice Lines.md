# Pro Voice Lines

A separate voice pack for every named pro. These lines are never shared with anyone else. Rules, events, playback and the generic vibe pools: [[Player Voice Lines]]. 3zzy's 86-line pack: [[Dev Players]].

- **Legendary/Epic packs:** 12 lines: SIGN, START, PLAY ×2, CLUTCH, WIN, LOSS, RIVAL, MATE, IDLE ×2, TRAIN.
- **Mythic packs:** the same 12 lines, plus **TRAIT ×2**, played when their Mythic unique triggers ([[Trait Library]]).
- Events a pack doesn't cover (e.g. TILT) fall back to the pro's **vibe** pool.
- Line ids: `VO_<TAG>_<EVENT>_<n>`. Example: thud's second PLAY line is `VO_THUD_PLAY_2`.
- Each pack header lists **title · role · rarity · vibe**, plus a **voice** direction for casting.
- Recording status per pack: ☐ not recorded · 🎙 recorded · ✅ uploaded.

All personalities are original. They copy playstyle archetypes, never a real pro's catchphrases ([[Pro Player References]] IP rule).

## Adding a new pro pack
1. Add the pro to [[Pro Player References]] or [[Monthly Seasons]] first.
2. Pick a vibe (fallback pool) and write a one-line voice direction.
3. Write 12 lines (14 for Mythics, with 2 TRAIT lines that fit their unique trait), following the [[Player Voice Lines]] writing rules.
4. Check no other pro already uses the same line or catchphrase.
5. Add the pack to `VoiceLines.lua` and the text to `VoiceText.lua`. The validation script checks the rest, including keeping 3zzy on top.
