## ★ Rare Special Items (knives and gloves)
Every Aim Arena weapon case (Pro, Elite, Legend, Arena, event weapon cases) has a **★ Rare Special** slot, CS2-style:
| Case | ★ chance per open | Pool |
|---|---|---|
| Pro Crate | 0.10% | Knives (base finishes) |
| Arena Crate / event weapon cases | 0.26% | Knives or gloves from that case's set |
| Elite Crate | 0.40% | Knives + gloves |
| Legend Crate | 0.80% | Knives + gloves, rare finishes (Fade, Galaxy, Lava Lamp phases) |
★ items roll seed, condition and finish like any skin ([[Skin Pattern System]]). The ★ chance shows in the Odds panel. Full list: [[Skin Library]] → Knives and gloves.

## Case mechanics ideas
- **Pity bar** per case family (Core / Themed / Event), not per individual case.
- **Key + case** split (P3): free case drops from matches, key bought with Gems. Simple to understand, but check Roblox policy first.
- **Bonus wheel**: every 10th open gives a free spin for a Booster (no currency in).
- **Rarity telegraph**: case glow color during the shake shows the lowest guaranteed rarity of that open.
- **Lucky streak**: 3 Epic+ in a row triggers a server announcement and a small Fame bonus.
- **Duplicate protection**: named Mythics/Legendaries can't drop twice until you've collected all in that case's pool.
- **Preview tray**: the Odds panel shows every possible item with a silhouette for unowned ones.
- **Open animation themes**: case-reveal stage skins from [[Microtransaction Ideas]] (volcano, space, arcade, stadium).
