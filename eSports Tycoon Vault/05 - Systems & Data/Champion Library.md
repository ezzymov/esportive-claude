# Champion Library (Tower Clash)

Champions are the characters roster players pick in [[Tower Clash]]. Unlike Aim Arena Agents, Champions **do matter**: the draft builds a team composition that gives small bonuses in the sim. Abilities are shown in cutscenes and clips.

## Design rules
- Each Champion: 1 class, 1–2 lanes, 2 draft tags, a passive, a signature ability and an ultimate (all visual in cutscenes).
- One clear theme per Champion (food spirit, toy soldier, troll…). Readable from the top-down camera.
- Stylized fantasy. No blood or gore; defeated Champions ragdoll and fade to their respawn.
- Names checked against LoL, Dota 2, Smite, Overwatch, Paladins and other hero games before shipping.

## Draft tags and team comps
Draft tags: **Engage** (starts fights) · **Peel** (protects allies) · **Poke** (damage from range) · **Burst** (quick big damage) · **Sustain** (heals/shields) · **Siege** (takes towers) · **Pick** (catches one target) · **Split** (pushes a side lane alone) · **Scaling** (strong late) · **Early** (strong early).

| Comp | Needs | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| **Dive** | 2+ Engage, 1+ Burst | +3% in teamfights |
| **Poke & Siege** | 2+ Poke, 1+ Siege | +3% tower damage, +2% vs Protect |
| **Protect the Carry** | 2+ Peel/Sustain, 1 Scaling Marksman | +3% late game |
| **Pick** | 2+ Pick | +3% to catch-outs before Titans |
| **Split Push** | 1 Split + 2 Poke/Siege | +3% when behind |

**Counters (light):** Dive beats Poke & Siege · Poke & Siege beats Protect the Carry · Protect the Carry beats Dive. Counter = extra +2%. The Analyst staff shows the opponent's likely comp during draft ([[Staff & Training]]).

## Champion pools for roster players
- Each roster player has **3 comfort Champions** (rolled by [[Player Generator]] from their role) with +5% when picked.
- Champion mastery rises with games played (0–100, up to +5% more).
- Banning an enemy player's comfort Champion is a real draft strategy.
